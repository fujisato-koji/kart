# Navigator

<a class="app-link" href="../navigator/">Open the navigator</a>

The navigator lets you browse the record as a web of linked items: requirements, values, decisions, open questions, hazards, sources and checks. Pick an item to see its text, its status and everything it cites or is cited by.

Its 3D mode shows the kart beside the selection. The parts of the kart are tied to the record's values and requirements by shared part ids: selecting an item lights its parts in 3D, and picking a part in 3D selects it in the record.

Every number and every statement in it comes from `record/` through `docs/data/record.json`. That file is generated by `python tools/gen_site_data.py`; nothing is typed into the app by hand.

Item ids in these pages (R-01, D-003, Q-009 and so on) link straight to the matching item in the navigator.
