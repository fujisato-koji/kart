# 1.4 Availability

What can be had, at what price and lead time, and what changes the design if it cannot.

Every part of 1.3 was looked for new in Japan (D-017): at MonotaRO, Misumi, Askul, Amazon.co.jp, Yahoo Shopping, Rakuten, kart shops, and steel stockists that cut to length for individuals. Prices and sources are in the parts list (1.5) and in the record.

## 1.4.1 Found as catalogue parts {#catalogue}

Bearings (P32, P33, M34, M47, M40), sprockets and chain of size 40 (P31, M45, M46), rod ends (P34, M38), steel tube, square tube, and flat bar cut to length (M6, M7, M55, M56), 13x5.00-6 wheels with tyres (M10), a hydraulic brake kit (M42), a pedal kit with a throttle cable (M43), stop switches (M50, M60), a seat with slide rails (M62), punched sheet and padding (M58, M59), and UHMW sheet for ski runners (M57).

## 1.4.2 Not found, made instead {#made}

These parts were not found for a 25 mm shaft in Japan, so they are made or machined by a local shop, priced as allowances until quoted (Q-018):

- the two rear hubs, keyed to the axle with a flange for the rim and a cross pin for the two track positions (1.3.3, 1.3.11);
- the brake disc carrier for a 25 mm shaft (sold only for a 1 inch shaft, M41);
- the 36 tooth axle sprocket, bought with a pilot bore and bored and keyed to 25 mm (M45);
- the keyway and pin holes on the axle and the keyway on the jackshaft.

## 1.4.3 To confirm when bought {#confirm}

Sellers do not state how these parts fit each other; each is checked on arrival before the frame is welded to it (Q-019): the rims' hole pattern against the hubs, the front hubs' bore against the 17 mm spindles, the engine's 3/4 inch shaft and base holes (P30 gives the Honda pattern), the ladder chains on 13x5.00-6 tyres (no chain of that size was found in Japan; the adjustable one is M15), and the seat's rail bolt spacing. The ATV parts of the cheaper list (D-033) are settled before ordering: the knuckles' mounting, the drum hubs' studs against the wheels, and the meter's lighting (Q-037).

## 1.4.4 Back to the design {#feedback}

- The engine has a 3/4 inch shaft, because every 10 tooth clutch for chain 40 sold in Japan has that bore (1.3.4).
- The rear axle is metric 25 mm, because the bearings, sprockets, and collars from Japanese makers are metric; this is why the hubs and the disc carrier are made (1.4.2).
- The 5-year-old's leg length has no public data; the fit check uses an assumed ratio (Q-017), and the pedal box's range is wide enough to cover it.
